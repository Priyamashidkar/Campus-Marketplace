How Campus Marketplace Operates

A seamless 4-step workflow connecting students and university ERP oversight.

1
Sign Up & List

Register with your verified student ID. Upload details & images of items you wish to sell.

2
Discover & Cart

Browse campus categories, filter by condition and price, and add desired items to your shopping cart.

3
Atomic Checkout

Select a secure campus pickup spot. ERP automatically updates stock and generates transaction logs.

4
Campus Pickup

Meet at the library or student center, inspect the item, complete handoff, and rate the seller.

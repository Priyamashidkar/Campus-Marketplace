Project Architecture

About Campus Marketplace ERP

An academic capstone system demonstrating tight coupling between student e-commerce transactions and university enterprise resource planning.

Peer-to-Peer E-Commerce

Enables university students to buy, sell, exchange and review pre-owned textbooks, lab kits, laptops, calculators, and hostel essentials without third-party fees.

  • Verified campus email identification
  • Real-time search, category filtering & sorting
  • Stock-aware shopping cart & atomic checkout

Integrated ERP Core

Directly coordinates inventory states, financial audit trails, and student dispute resolution across a centralized relational database.

  • Automated inventory decrements & stock health
  • Low-stock alerts & admin replenishment controls
  • Centralized complaint ticketing & resolution

Dynamic Analytics

Visualizes real-time marketplace demand, monthly sales patterns, and product category trends using interactive Chart.js visualizations.

  • Monthly sales volume & revenue trajectories
  • Product category distribution analysis
  • Top-selling products and active seller rankings

Campus Trading & Safety Guidelines

Official Campus Meeting Points

Always schedule your handovers at well-lit, populated campus hubs such as the Central Library Ground Floor, Student Activity Center, or Department Lounges.

Inspect Before Completing

Inspect electronic devices, book editions, and hardware thoroughly prior to releasing payment or accepting completion.

Simulated Academic Payment

All transaction figures on this platform are simulated for demonstration purposes and academic evaluation.

Dispute Resolution

If an item does not match its listing description, file a ticket under "My Complaints" for administrative investigation.
